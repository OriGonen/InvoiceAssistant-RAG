# Payment Terms (SAMPLE DATA - illustrative only, not legal or tax advice)

## Default payment deadline
If no deadline is agreed, an invoice is due 30 days after the issue date.

## Maximum agreed deadline
Between businesses, the agreed payment deadline should not exceed 60 days unless both parties explicitly agree and it is not unfair to the creditor.

## Late payment interest
If an invoice is not paid by its due date, the supplier may charge statutory default interest from the first day after the due date, without sending a reminder first.

## Reminders
Good practice is to send a first reminder 7 days after the due date and a second reminder after 21 days, before handing the claim to collection.
