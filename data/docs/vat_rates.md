# VAT Rates

## Standard rate
The standard VAT rate is 22%. It applies to most goods and services unless a reduced rate or exemption is listed below.

## Reduced rate
A reduced rate of 9.5% applies to food, drinking water, passenger transport, accommodation services and some agricultural supplies.

## Super-reduced rate
A rate of 5% applies to printed and electronic books, newspapers and magazines.

## Exempt supplies
Healthcare, education, insurance and most financial services are exempt from VAT. No VAT is charged, but input VAT on related costs usually cannot be deducted.

## Registration threshold
In this sample ruleset, a business must register for VAT once its taxable turnover exceeds 50,000 EUR in the last 12 months.
