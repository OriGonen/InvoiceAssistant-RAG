# Invoice Requirements

## Mandatory fields
Every invoice must include: a sequential invoice number, the issue date, the supply date, the seller's and buyer's names and addresses, the seller's VAT number, a description of goods or services, the net amount, the VAT rate and VAT amount, and the total amount due.

## Invoice numbering
Invoice numbers must be sequential and unique, with no gaps. A common format is year plus sequence, for example 2026-0042.

## When to issue
An invoice should be issued no later than the 10th day of the month following the month of supply.

## Currency
Invoices may be issued in any currency, but the VAT amount must also be stated in euros.

## Record keeping
Issued and received invoices must be kept for at least 10 years after the end of the year they relate to.
