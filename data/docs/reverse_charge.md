# Reverse Charge

## What reverse charge means
Under the reverse-charge mechanism, the buyer, not the seller, accounts for the VAT. The seller issues the invoice without VAT, and the buyer calculates and reports the VAT in their own VAT return.

## When it applies
Reverse charge typically applies to business-to-business services supplied to a customer registered for VAT in another EU member state, and to certain domestic supplies such as construction services.

## Invoice requirements under reverse charge
The invoice must show the VAT identification numbers of both seller and buyer, must not include a VAT amount, and must include the note "Reverse charge" (Slovenian: "Obrnjena davčna obveznost").

## Checking the buyer's VAT number
Before applying reverse charge to an EU customer, the seller should verify the buyer's VAT number in the EU VIES system and keep a record of the check.
